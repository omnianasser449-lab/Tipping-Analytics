📊 Restaurant Gratuity & Tipping Analytics
The Goal
Identify the key behavioral and environmental drivers of tipping to optimize restaurant staffing and server revenue.

🛠️ Tools Used
Python (Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib)
Machine Learning: Linear Regression (Predictive Modeling)
💡 Key Insights
The "Friday" Premium: Friday emerged as the most significant day for tipping (Weight: +0.10), while Saturdays showed a relative decrease in tipping efficiency.
Predictive Accuracy: The model achieved a Mean Absolute Error (MAE) of $0.67, meaning it can predict tips with high precision.
Group Dynamics: Found that for every additional person at a table (size), the tip increases by approximately $0.23 on average.
The "Total Bill" Impact: For every 1.00 increase in the bill ,the tipping increases by roughly 0.09.
🚀 Strategy Recommendation
Maximize server ROI by prioritizing senior staff for Friday shifts and large-party reservations (Size 4+). Since the total bill is a primary driver, focus training on upselling high-margin appetizers and desserts to automatically boost gratuity amounts.
